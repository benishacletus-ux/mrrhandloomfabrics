Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our products and services

What is the mimimum order quantity (MOQ)?

The minimum order quantity (MOQ) is 6 piece.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes. We offer wholesale pricing directly from the manufacturer. Prices may vary depending on the product and quantity.

How can I place a wholesale order?

Browse our website and choose the products you’re interested in. Take a screenshot of the product and send it to us on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm the wholesale price, availability, and order details.

Do you ship across India?

Yes. We provide Pan India delivery through reliable courier services.

Can I request custom or bulk orders?

Yes. Bulk orders and custom requirements can be discussed depending on the product, quantity and availability. Please contact us for details.

What is your return and exchange policy?

Returns or exchanges are accepted only for damaged, defective or incorrect products, subject to our return policy. Customers are requested to share an unboxing video for any issue with the order.

About Us

Preserving the legacy of Bhagalpuri handloom since 2020

Our Story

MRR Handloom Fabrics is a Bhagalpur-based manufacturer and supplier of premium handloom sarees, fabrics, suit materials and dupattas.

Established in 2020, our journey started with a vision to bring authentic Bhagalpuri handloom products directly from the manufacturer to retailers, boutiques, resellers and wholesalers across India.

We focus on quality, attractive designs, competitive wholesale pricing and reliable service. Our goal is to build long-term relationships with our customers while promoting the rich handloom heritage of Bhagalpur.
